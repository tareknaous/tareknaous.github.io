WWE Bad Blood, Atlanta, GA - Oct 2024

I grew up in Lebanon watching WWE with my brothers and cousins. This was my first time attending a WWE event when they held the Bad Blood 2024 PPV in Atlanta. It was a surreal experience to witness wrestling entertainment live and see famous superstars like CM Punk and Triple H. This event was also special since it had a Hell in a Cell match which they usually do only once a year. Also got to see Cody Rhodes jump from the ring post and break the announce table.
